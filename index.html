About me


I’m Flor — a UX designer with a strong visual focus, and a front-end developer. I love crafting digital experiences that not only work, but connect. I enjoy understanding people’s needs and turning them into visual ideas. From the first sketch to the final pixel, I aim to create experiences that spark smiles, build loyalty, and feel truly relatable.

Squad Goals: Group Saving App


A collaborative budgeting app for friends and families, designed to simplify household expenses and make saving toward shared goals (like a trip or emergency fund) more transparent and motivating.


This UX case study includes research, wireframes, interactive prototypes, and usability testing. Created as part of a UX Design course on Coursera.

Sher Beauty – Visual Identity & Social Media Design


I designed the complete visual identity for Sher Beauty. The project included a custom logo, Instagram feed design, digital gift cards, loyalty cards, and a business card. The use of bold red and organic shapes reflects the brand's focus on self-care and precision. My goal was achieving a cohesive and recognizable look across both digital and print formats, strengthening brand presence and client connection.

Team Project: e-commerce


This e-commerce site was created as a group project by 5 participants during the Jóvenes a Programar 2024 course. We collaborated on design, development, and structure, each contributing different parts to the final product. Some of my contributions where: designed and implemented the User Profile section, Contributed to JavaScript functionality in various parts of the site, Helped with low- and high-fidelity prototypes (Figma).


This project was part of a learning experience and is shared here for educational purposes only. All rights belong to Ceibal and the Jóvenes a Programar program.
